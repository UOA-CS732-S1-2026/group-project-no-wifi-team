# Quarter Themes and Events

> Source: Quarter Theme+Event+Random Even.docx

Game structure: Each quarter contains tasks from three categories: Study, Leisure, and Social. Each task gives the player multiple choices, and each choice changes the player’s attributes in a different way.


## Semester 1: Orientation and Adjustment — Study Tasks


### Level 1 - Course Selection Background: A new semester begins, and it is time to choose your courses. You decide to...

| Choice | Effect | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Optimise Your Timetable | Intelligence:+1 | You dive into course requirements, timetable clashes, and every rumour on student forums. At last, you build a timetable that looks almost suspiciously responsible. |
| Choose Morning Classes | Health:+1 | You pick morning classes to force yourself into a healthier routine. Somehow, your alarm clock becomes both your worst enemy and your life coach. |
| Choose Benefit-Friendly Courses | Wealth:+1 | You choose courses that line up with scholarship points and funding requirements. Education is important, but so is not eating instant noodles forever. |
| Wing It | None | You do zero research and pick a few courses from the list like you are ordering from a menu you cannot read. |

| Go for Leadership | Intelligence:+2,Health:-1 | You take on a leadership role. It looks great on paper, but your free time quietly files a missing-person report. |
| Skip the Clubs | None | You skip every club and enjoy your own company. Peaceful, efficient, and only slightly suspicious. |

| Choice | Effect | Description |
